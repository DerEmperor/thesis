\section{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence}

I hereby declare that Generative AI tools were utilized during the research and preparation of this Master's thesis. Their application was strictly limited to the following parameters:

\begin{description}
	\item[Language \& Style Refinement (Google Gemini 3.6 \& OpenAI ChatGPT 5.5)]
	These models were used exclusively to assist with stylistic polishing, sentence restructuring, rephrasing, and grammatical corrections across selected chapters. No core concepts, original arguments, or textual content were generated by these models.
	
	\item[Source Organization \& Literature Synthesis (Google NotebookLM)]
	This tool was employed as a research assistant to organize, summarize, and cross-reference uploaded source materials and literature notes. 
\end{description}

\vspace{1em}

No core ideas, research designs, empirical data analyses, or final conclusions were produced by artificial intelligence. All literature cited within this thesis was independently reviewed, evaluated, and verified by the author. I retain full authorship, responsibility, and accountability for the content, accuracy, and academic integrity of this work.
